## KX documentation

Thank you to KX Systems and the documentation contributors for making their q reference available under an open licence.

The files in the `basics` and `ref` sections were imported from
[Documentation for kdb+ and q](https://github.com/KxSystems/docs), by KX Systems
and contributors, through [Ryan Hamilton's fork](https://github.com/ryanhamilton/docs)
at commit [`508a1ae8c8309ac9628aad4f61b8bd8d1d127aeb`](https://github.com/ryanhamilton/docs/commit/508a1ae8c8309ac9628aad4f61b8bd8d1d127aeb).

The material is used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
PeachQ imported only the `docs/basics` and `docs/ref` sections. The initial
snapshot is unmodified; later PeachQ changes are recorded in this repository's
Git history.

PeachQ is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by KX. KX and kdb+ are trademarks of their respective owners.
