On-disk databases: what works today

Flat files and splayed tables work: PeachQ reads and writes them in the kdb+ on-disk format, including compressed splayed tables when reading. Partitioned and segmented databases do not load yet, and .Q.dpft, dsave and partitioned writes are not available. For a typical historical database (HDB) this is the largest gap today; see the details.
