    cor¶

    Correlation

    x cor y    cor[x;y]

    Where x and y are conforming numeric lists, returns their (Pearson) correlation as a float in the range -1f to 1f. Nulls (along with their pairs) are ignored.

    q)29 10 54 cor 1 3 9
0.7727746
q)10 29 54 cor 1 3 9
0.9795734
q)1 3 9 cor 1 3 9
1f
q)1 3 9 cor neg 1 3 9
-1f
q)1 3 1 3 cor 1 1 3 3
0f
q)1 1 1 cor 1 3 9
0n
q)1 3 0N cor 1 3 9                  /nulls are ignored
1f

q)1000101000b cor 0010011001b
-0.08908708

    cor is an aggregate function, equivalent to {cov[x;y]%dev[x]*dev y}.
